Projectile motion

A launched object follows a curved path when gravity is the only acceleration. Horizontal velocity stays constant in this ideal model.

H = u² sin²θ / 2g
  • Horizontal and vertical motion are independent.
  • Air resistance is ignored.
  • At the highest point, vertical velocity is zero.
